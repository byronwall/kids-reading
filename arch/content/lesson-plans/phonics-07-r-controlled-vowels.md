# R-Controlled Vowels

## Chunk Goals

- Recognize how r changes the sound of a nearby vowel.
- Read ar, or, er, ir, and ur spellings.
- Compare several spellings that share an r-controlled sound.

#### Teacher Note

Have the learner notice the a and r working together to make the ar sound. Ask for a slow blend followed by a smooth reread. Listen for a full r-controlled vowel rather than separate short a and r sounds.
