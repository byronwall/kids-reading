# Magic-E Words

## Chunk Goals

- Recognize final e as a signal for a long vowel.
- Contrast short-vowel words with related magic-e words.
- Read long-vowel words smoothly in controlled sentences.

#### Teacher Note

Have the learner notice the final e making the earlier a say its long sound. Ask for a slow blend followed by a smooth reread. Listen for the long a sound while the final e remains silent.
