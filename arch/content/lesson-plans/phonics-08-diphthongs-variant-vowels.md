# Diphthongs and Variant Vowels

## Chunk Goals

- Read vowel sounds that glide or vary from basic short and long vowels.
- Compare alternate spellings for oi, ou, aw, and oo sounds.
- Apply variant-vowel patterns in controlled sentences.

#### Teacher Note

Have the learner notice oi usually appearing inside words and oy often appearing at the end. Ask for a slow blend followed by a smooth reread. Listen for the gliding vowel sound remaining intact instead of splitting into two sounds.
