# Long-Vowel Teams

## Chunk Goals

- Recognize common letter teams that represent long vowels.
- Compare alternate spellings for the same long-vowel sound.
- Read vowel-team words in increasingly fluent sentences.

#### Practice Sentences

```text

I can play.

The sail is on the bay.

The main train can stay.

```

#### Teacher Note

Have the learner notice ai often appearing inside a word and ay often appearing at the end. Ask for a slow blend followed by a smooth reread. Listen for the same long a sound across both spellings.
