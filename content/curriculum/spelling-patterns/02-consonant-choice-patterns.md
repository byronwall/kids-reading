# Consonant Choice Patterns

## Chunk Goals

- Compare hard and soft sounds for c and g.
- Use the letters around c and g to predict their sounds.
- Read and spell words with doubled consonants in controlled sentences.

#### Teacher Note

#### Teacher Note

Have the learner notice doubled consonants at the ends of short words and inside longer words. Ask the learner to read the vowel before the doubled letters without adding a second consonant sound. Use word sorts to compare ff, ll, ss, zz, and medial doubled consonants.
