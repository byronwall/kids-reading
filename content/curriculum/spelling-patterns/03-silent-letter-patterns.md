# Silent Letter Patterns

## Chunk Goals

- Recognize silent letters in common kn and wr words.
- Read words with silent l, gh, and t spellings.
- Review silent-letter patterns in connected text.

## Lessons

#### Practice Sentences

```text
lesson-23-01-sentence-01 | I know the knot is on the wrapper.
lesson-23-01-sentence-02 | The wren is by the wreath.
lesson-23-01-sentence-03 | I can knit a wrap.
lesson-23-01-sentence-04 | The knob is on the knapsack.
lesson-23-01-sentence-05 | The wrong wrapper is by the wreck.
lesson-23-01-sentence-06 | The wrench is in the knapsack.
lesson-23-01-sentence-07 | I can kneel by the knoll.
lesson-23-01-sentence-08 | The rabbit can wrestle the wrong wren.
lesson-23-01-sentence-09 | The knot can wring the wrap.
lesson-23-01-sentence-10 | The knuckle is by the shell.
lesson-23-01-sentence-11 | I wrote the knack on the wrapper.
lesson-23-01-sentence-12 | The wrung wrap is by the knee.
```

#### Teacher Note

Have the learner notice that k is silent in kn words and w is silent in wr words. Ask the learner to start with the n or r sound, then blend the rest of the word. Remind the learner that the first letter still helps show the word family.
